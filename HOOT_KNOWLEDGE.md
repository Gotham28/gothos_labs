## ELogBook

ELogBook is a digital logbook for postgraduate medical training. It gives residents, faculty, and Heads of Department (HODs) different views of training work.

- Residents can record clinical cases they observed, assisted with, or managed; procedures and supervision levels; academic activities such as seminars, journal clubs, presentations, and teaching; and postings and rotations. They can view training progress, requirements, assessments, and feedback.
- Faculty can review and verify submitted entries and follow the progress of assigned residents. HODs can review department submissions, manage assessments and faculty access, oversee leave approvals, and configure case, procedure, and academic requirements.
- Depending on department configuration, the logbook can include attendance, leave records, thesis and research work, publications, certifications, conferences, and other training sections. Names and availability of sections differ by department. A printable logbook view is available.

Do not imply that every department has every section or that Mochi can access resident records. The site's ELogBook preview is illustrative, not a live resident account.

## AROGYA

AROGYA is a separate AI assistant for hospitals. It gives answers using each hospital's own configured information. It can help visitors find doctors by department, check availability, and submit appointment requests. Where a hospital has supplied the relevant data, it can answer questions about its timings, services, contact information, medicines, and lab tests. Do not promise these optional answers for every hospital.

AROGYA supports English and Malayalam. Its web chat supports typed questions and microphone input. Hospital branding and information can be configured, and the product has WhatsApp integration. Do not promise a specific WhatsApp notification or patient reminder without checking that hospital's setup.

An appointment request receives a reference, but the hospital calls to confirm the slot. Do not call it an instant confirmed appointment. The website states that a hospital's data never appears in another hospital's answers. The site says AROGYA is in live use at a hospital in Kerala, without naming it. Mochi is Gothos Labs' website guide, not AROGYA; Mochi cannot look up hospital records, doctor availability, appointments, or patient information. This website does not yet have a configured AROGYA live demo link.
