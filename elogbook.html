ELogBook

A digital logbook for postgraduate medical training.

Residents log cases, procedures and academic work, professors review them, and Heads of Department track progress against training targets.

Request a demo Talk to us

Currently in pilot with the Pediatrics department

The problem

Paper logbooks get lost, are reviewed late, and make it hard for a department to see where residents stand against requirements.

How it works

Residents: log clinical cases, procedures and academic activities, and see progress against targets.

Key features

  • Case, procedure and academic logs
  • Professor review and approval
  • An HOD portal
  • Leave and attendance records
  • Postings and research tracking
  • Per-department configuration, not hardcoded to one specialty

Privacy and access

Residents see their own records, professors see their assigned residents, and HODs see their department.
