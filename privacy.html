Website Privacy Policy

How Gothos Lab Private Limited handles information when you visit the Gothos Labs website, contact us, or use Mochi.

Draft for review. Production data handling and retention settings are pending confirmation.

What we use and why

Contact and demo enquiries

When you send an enquiry, we use your name if you provide it, your email address, your selected topic, and your message to respond. We attach a reference and a timestamp so the team can identify the enquiry. If you request a demo through Mochi, it asks for your name and email and sends the enquiry after you confirm. We use Resend to send these enquiries by email.

Questions for Mochi

When you send a question to Mochi for an AI reply, your question and recent conversation context go to OpenAI to generate the response. Closing Mochi clears the conversation from the current page. Email us about information that has already been sent. Please leave out patient records, health information, passwords, and personal information about other people.

Site operation

The website uses network address information to limit abusive requests. Your browser stores your motion preference for the current session.

Under review. We are confirming which standard request logs our hosting provider keeps and for how long. This section will be updated once confirmed.

Keeping and deleting information

Information sent to the team or to a service provider can be held separately from your browser session. Email us if you want us to check what information is held about you or request deletion. We’ll review your request and explain any applicable reason that information must be kept.

Privacy requests and grievances

Email gothoslabs@gmail.com to ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent where applicable, ask about nomination, or raise a privacy grievance.

Tell us what you’d like us to do. For an enquiry, include the email address you used and the enquiry reference if you have it. We may ask for enough information to verify the request before disclosing or changing data. Please avoid sending identity documents or sensitive records with your initial email.

Raise a grievance with us first. If it remains unresolved, you may have a right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India once the applicable provisions and complaint process are in effect.

Which services this covers

This policy covers the public Gothos Labs website, including the ELogBook and AROGYA information pages and their website enquiry links. For data handled in a hospital’s or department’s live service, consult the notice provided with that service.
