What we do

We bring AI to your software, so it works better.

Gothos Labs builds AI into the software people already rely on, so everyday work gets simpler and better. We start from a real workflow, build with the people who use it, and keep each organisation's information its own. ELogBook and AROGYA are where we started: one for postgraduate medical training, one for hospitals and their patients.

ELogBook

A digital logbook for postgraduate medical training.

Residents log their work, professors review it, and Heads of Department track progress against training targets.

Explore ELogBook

AROGYA

An AI assistant that speaks your patients' language.

Answers in English and Malayalam, helps patients find doctors and book appointments, on the web and on WhatsApp.

General information only. It does not diagnose or replace a doctor. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
