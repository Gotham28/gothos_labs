## Decisions for the operator

1. Confirm the actual production data recipients, including the approved enquiry mailbox, the person responsible for the privacy inbox, and their backup. Confirm provider roles, access, contracts, hosting regions, retention and available deletion controls. Keep credentials and private account details in the approved restricted workspace.
2. Approve the legal basis separately for each flow. Consider section 7(a) for responding to a voluntarily submitted enquiry for its stated purpose. Assess AI chat separately. If consent is the selected basis, approve the specific notice, affirmative action, evidence of the notice and consent, and a withdrawal path comparable to the way it was given. Enquiry submission and demo confirmation cover their stated purposes. Assess the basis for any additional use separately. [DPDP Act, sections 4–7](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf)
3. Confirm how visitors can access applicable notice and consent language options. Review the public chat audience and how to handle child data and unsolicited sensitive information. The current medical refusal is a limited keyword check. Treat handling patient data and child data as separate requirements to validate. [DPDP Act, sections 5, 6 and 9](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf)
4. Approve a retention schedule for the enquiry inbox, provider records, hosting and security logs, backups, and privacy requests. Record the purpose, event that starts the retention period, owner, exceptions and deletion procedure for each. ## Privacy requests and grievances

1. The designated owner checks the privacy inbox, acknowledges the request, and records a restricted case reference, date, category, owner and status. Agree an operational response period before publishing it. Review Rule 14’s grievance response requirement when applicable.
2. Ask for the enquiry reference, the email address used or other information proportionate to the request. Verify the requester or authorised representative before releasing or modifying information. Ask for identity documents only if necessary and use an approved secure channel.
3. Locate relevant data in the authorised mailbox, hosting records and provider accounts as applicable. Check the approved legal and retention schedule. Handle access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal where applicable, nomination and grievances according to the verified workflow. Forward a processor request only through its approved channel and share the minimum necessary data.
4. Record the outcome, any remaining copies or lawful restriction, and how the requester can follow up. Confirm processor deletion or record its limitations. If a grievance remains unresolved, provide the verified Board escalation route when available and applicable. [DPDP Act, sections 11–14](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf) · [DPDP Rules, Rules 9 and 14](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

## Incident escalation

If anyone suspects an accidental disclosure, unexpected mailbox delivery, or provider incident, alert the designated incident owner immediately. Contain the issue, restrict access, preserve the minimum evidence securely, document the affected data and people, and involve the responsible operator and provider. Verify current notification duties and channels. When the DPDP breach rule applies, it calls for prompt notice to affected people and the Board, with a detailed update to the Board within 72 hours unless the Board allows longer. [DPDP Rules, Rule 7](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)
